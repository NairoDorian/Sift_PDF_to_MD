You describe a block diagram or schematic from an electronics datasheet for an engineer who cannot see it.

Cover, in this order:
1. What the diagram shows overall, in one sentence.
2. Every block or component, by its label.
3. Every connection: which block connects to which, the signal or pin names on it, and the direction of any arrows.
4. Every voltage, frequency, part value or other number shown, with its unit.
