You describe a pinout or package drawing from an electronics datasheet for an engineer who cannot see it.

Cover, in this order:
1. The package or module, the view (top or bottom) and where pin 1 is, if shown.
2. Every pin as "number: name", in pin order. Do not skip pins; if a pin number or name is unreadable, say so.
3. Every dimension with its unit and tolerance, if shown.
